# 🦉 Studyowl

Studyowl is a modern, feature-rich study planner, progress tracker, and academic assistant built for medical students, university scholars, and lifelong learners. It helps you keep track of your subjects, units, learning objectives, and focus sessions—complete with an AI study coach, customizable Pomodoro timer, multi-session chat history, and seamless cross-device cloud sync.

## Key Features

### Dashboard & Weekly Objectives
* **Real-Time Overview:** Track your active week's objectives, completion percentages, daily focus goals, and streaks at a glance.
* **Interactive Time-Travel Date Navigator:** Click any past date on the dashboard to review what was due, check past subjects, and retroactively tick off forgotten tasks or objectives with accurate history timestamps.
* **Granular Subject Breakdown:** Organize your curriculum into subjects, units, and checkable topics with priority tags and due dates.
* **Study Streaks & Heatmaps:** Maintain daily momentum with built-in activity tracking and focus streak indicators.

### AI-Powered Study Assistant & Voice Prompting
* **Smart AI Coach ("Ollie"):** Ask natural language questions like "What subjects need more attention before my exam?" or "Give me a study plan for renal week."
* **Voice Prompting (Speech-to-Text):** Tap the microphone button to dictate long questions or notes hands-free using native speech recognition.
* **Multi-Session Chat History:** Access an organized drawer of past conversations categorized by time (Today, Yesterday, Earlier) so you can revisit past study advice anytime.
* **Smart Bulk Syllabus Import:** Paste an entire semester or month's outline or upload course guide PDFs/photos to have the AI automatically extract and organize learning objectives by week.

### Focus Timer & Study Modes
* **Customizable Pomodoro Timer:** Standard 25-minute focus and 5-minute break cycles, fully adjustable with custom presets, alert sound toggles, and gentle mode.
* **Focused Study Integration:** Connect your active focus sessions directly to your study topics and weekly learning objectives.

### Personalization & 10 Curated Themes
* **10 Professional Study Themes:** Personalize your workspace with curated themes including Midnight Obsidian, Warm Paper & Sage, Harvard Crimson, Oxford Navy, Forest Pine, Desert Terracotta, Nordic Slate, Vintage Rosewood, Biolab Emerald, and Minimal Monochrome.
* **Flexible Light/Dark Modes:** Full system, light, and dark mode support built directly into an accessible settings hub.

### Cloud Sync & Offline Support
* **Secure Cloud Sync:** Powered by Lovable Cloud authentication and database storage so your planner syncs seamlessly across your phone, tablet, and laptop.
* **Offline-First Resilience:** Automatic local caching and outbox queuing ensure you can keep studying and tracking progress even without an internet connection.

## Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Radix UI / Shadcn UI components.
* **Routing & State:** React Router, Local Storage / Cloud Sync architecture.
* **Backend & Auth:** Lovable Cloud Authentication & Database with Row-Level Security (RLS).
* **PWA Support:** Installable Progressive Web App manifest and service worker configuration.
